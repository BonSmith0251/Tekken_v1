CHARACTER MUSEUM - ALL EIGHT FIGHTERS

The character list includes Jin Kazama, Kazuya Mishima, Ling Xiaoyu, Alisa, Katarina,
Lidia, Bob and Leo (assets under Assets/_Project/Museum). The notes below were written
when the museum had its first three fighters and describe those in detail; the other
five follow the same tools. Their outfits, wardrobes and colours are in WARDROBE.txt.

Select Ling Xiaoyu to view her Default 1P, Classic, Formal and School Uniform
outfits. Her catalog contains 2,437 original move records, 1,462 distinct body
clips and 9,020 cancel branches. Her own stance is selected when she loads.
The Staging category includes five entrances and four victory variants. All
nine have native hand tracks; eight also have native facial tracks and cameras.
Enable Original action camera for those eight takes, or leave it off to orbit
and scrub manually. The paired fu_w01_y variant has no independent face/camera
event and uses the museum camera and bind face. All four outfits now have adapted
hair/accessory springs and constrained cloth on garment hems, with body collision
capsules. Motion responds during playback and settles when paused. Seeking clears
stale inertia. These are Unity simulation settings rather than the original
Unreal physics. Combat finger/facial events, attachments and synchronized throw
partners are not integrated. The four outfits are complete imported sets, not
every customization.

Kazuya includes
eight complete installed outfits, with Karate Gi first for a clear body preview:
Karate Gi, Default 1P Silver Coat, Battle Damaged Gi, Tekken 4 Gi, Gilet,
Kamikaze, Tattoo Shirt, and Wagara. His silver coat intentionally hangs from
his shoulders with empty sleeves behind his fighting arms. He has 2,333
extracted move records, covering
1,378 distinct body clips and 9,683 cancel/transition entries. Select Kazuya in
the CHARACTERS list, then use the same search, playback, scrub and branch tools.

Launch Open Character Museum.cmd or open CharacterMuseum.unity and press Play.
Jin now has all 2,227 extracted move records, including transitions, variants,
shared reactions, item actions, throws, intros and outros. These reference
1,361 distinct body animations; shared animations remain separate move records.

Use the COSTUMES list to switch among Jin's seven complete installed outfit sets:
Default 1P, Karate Gi, Emperor, School Uniform, Long Coat, Regulus, and
Tattered / Devil. Costume changes keep the selected action and timeline frame,
so the same pose can be compared across every outfit. These are Jin-specific
full-body sets; the game's separate generic customization accessories are not
treated as complete costumes in this selector.

Search by original internal move name, animation name or numeric move ID.
Use the CATEGORY control to filter each character's records into Staging, Entrance,
Ready, Idle, Movement, Fighting, Throws, Reactions, Transitions, Victory, and
Other. The arrows move backward or forward through the categories; clicking
the category name also advances to the next one. Search applies inside the
selected category, while All searches the full character catalog.
Use the page arrows to browse all results, 100 records per page.
Space plays/pauses. Arrow keys step. Drag the timeline to scrub.
Right-drag orbits; mouse wheel zooms. Speed and looping are adjustable.
Source travel restores the clip's horizontal movement and makes the camera follow.

Transitions / original record opens the selected move's default transition,
all cancel branches, their input/window labels, and the full source record.
Click a branch to inspect its target. Branches are manual previews: the museum
does not evaluate original-game conditions or recreate its combat engine.

Jin's 8,553 cancel entries and the source conditions/properties are preserved.
The 124 player-facing movelist entries and input demos are available separately
in References/OriginalJinMotion/movelist.json; they do not supply direct animation IDs.

Coverage: all referenced body motions converted with mapping v3. Clips with
additional attachment channels display their body motion only. Object animation,
combat finger/facial animation, effects, and synchronized second-character throw
playback are not reconstructed here. Xiaoyu's staged hand/face tracks are the
exception described above. Numerical/frame validation is not a visual comparison
of every move against the original game.

Each MuseumCharacter asset owns its model, bone mapping and actions. Add other
characters after importing their models and converting their own motion libraries.

Use STAGE above the playback controls to cycle through the gallery and the 34
imported original geometry layouts. Stage materials, lighting and effects remain
neutral previews. Changing stages keeps the current character, costume and pose.

Xiaoyu skirt repair: School and Formal use a live waist suspension so thigh
helper rotations do not pull their hems sideways. Original imported meshes and
skin weights remain saved intact; only the live garment weights are adapted.
Waistband panels stay pinned, lower fabric has outfit-specific drape and thigh
collision clearance, and short-skirt materials render both sides. This is adapted
Unity cloth, not the original Unreal simulation. Dynamics checks run at 60 fps
across all four outfits, including attacks and timeline seeks.
