Open Assets/_Project/Scenes/staging_test.unity and press Play.

The scene reads StagingCatalog.json and plays each available character's
entrances, then victories, in move ID order. Only one actor is visible at a
time. Each take uses its own converted body, hand/face, and camera resources.
There is a short pause between takes, then the playlist repeats. Press Space
to pause; use the left and right arrows to change takes.

Current playable coverage: Jin (9 takes), Kazuya (10 takes) and Xiaoyu (9 takes:
five entrances, four victories). Xiaoyu uses her Default 1P outfit with the same
adapted hair springs and garment cloth as the Character Museum. Her fu_w01_y
paired victory has native hands but no source face or camera track.
Paired victory records without a source camera use a labeled frontal
fallback. The on-screen caption states when that fallback is active.
