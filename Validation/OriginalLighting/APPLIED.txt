ORIGINAL STG29 LIGHTING IN HDRP

Source: installed Tekken 7 cooked stg29 assets. See FINDINGS.txt and
stg29-lighting.json for original properties, provenance, and omitted fields.

Applied to versus scenes automatically by HdrpSceneLighting:
- Four directional lights, original RGB values, intensity ratios, and relative
  emission directions. UE (x,y,z) becomes Unity (x,z,-y).
- Three character lights use rendering layer 2 and do not cast shadows.
  Fighters receive layers 1 and 2; arena receives layer 1.
- Original HDR sky: six 1024-square RGBAHalf faces, reoriented without clipping,
  with mipmaps; used for background, ambient lighting and reflection fallback.
- Fog disabled; fixed exposure; lens flare disabled.
- HDRP SSR and light layers enabled in the pipeline asset.
- Camera lens, transforms and entrance sequence are unchanged.

Cross-renderer mappings (calibration choices, not exact UE equivalence):
- Native directional intensity -> lux at scale 1. Original ratios retained.
- UE exposure brightness min/max 1 -> HDRP fixed EV 0.
- UE bloom .2 -> HDRP intensity .2, scatter .7, threshold 1. UE convolution
  sizes/tints have no direct equivalent in this profile.
- UE AO radius 60/power 5 -> HDRP radius .6m/intensity 1. Original radius-space
  default was not serialized, so this is explicitly an approximation.
- UE SSR intensity 70 -> SSR enabled and reflection multiplier .7. The latter
  affects all reflections, not only SSR; smoothness thresholds .55/.8.
- Neutral tonemapping chosen; original tone curve was not fully recovered.
- Motion blur and depth of field disabled: stored source values did not prove
  those effects were active. KO/Rage-only grading is not applied globally.
- Environment light shadows enabled (inherited source flag unresolved).
- Light directions use extracted relative rotations; dynamic parent-follow
  behavior and baked local reflection capture data have not been reproduced.

Rebuild: python Tools/OriginalLighting/extract_stage_lighting.py, then
python Tools/OriginalLighting/prepare_hdrp_sky.py (requires numpy).
In Unity: Remaster > Rendering > Apply original stg29 lighting.

Validation: Applied/ contains fresh actual-playback captures of Kazuya, Jin,
and combat, plus runtime checks for HDRP, materials, lights, character layers,
fog, fixed exposure and HDR sky resolution. Before/ preserves generic HDRP
lighting for comparison. Stage geometry and character costumes still differ
from the original reference; this is not a pixel-identical stage reconstruction.
