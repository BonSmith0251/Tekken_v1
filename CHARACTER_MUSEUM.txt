CHARACTER MUSEUM - JIN FULL MOVE RECORDS

The character list now includes Jin Kazama and Kazuya Mishima. Kazuya includes
eight complete installed outfits, with Karate Gi first for a clear body preview:
Karate Gi, Default 1P Silver Coat, Battle Damaged Gi, Tekken 4 Gi, Gilet,
Kamikaze, Tattoo Shirt, and Wagara. His silver coat intentionally hangs from
his shoulders with empty sleeves behind his fighting arms. He has 2,333
extracted move records, covering
1,378 distinct body clips and 9,683 cancel/transition entries. Select Kazuya in
the CHARACTERS list, then use the same search, playback, scrub and branch tools.

Launch Open Character Museum.cmd or open CharacterMuseum.unity and press Play.
Jin now has all 2,227 extracted move records, including transitions, variants,
shared reactions, item actions, throws, intros and outros. These reference
1,361 distinct body animations; shared animations remain separate move records.

Use the COSTUMES list to switch among Jin's seven complete installed outfit sets:
Default 1P, Karate Gi, Emperor, School Uniform, Long Coat, Regulus, and
Tattered / Devil. Costume changes keep the selected action and timeline frame,
so the same pose can be compared across every outfit. These are Jin-specific
full-body sets; the game's separate generic customization accessories are not
treated as complete costumes in this selector.

Search by original internal move name, animation name or numeric move ID.
Use the page arrows to browse all results, 100 records per page.
Space plays/pauses. Arrow keys step. Drag the timeline to scrub.
Right-drag orbits; mouse wheel zooms. Speed and looping are adjustable.
Source travel restores the clip's horizontal movement and makes the camera follow.

Transitions / original record opens the selected move's default transition,
all cancel branches, their input/window labels, and the full source record.
Click a branch to inspect its target. Branches are manual previews: the museum
does not evaluate original-game conditions or recreate its combat engine.

All 8,553 cancel entries and the source conditions/properties are preserved.
The 124 player-facing movelist entries and input demos are available separately
in References/OriginalJinMotion/movelist.json; they do not supply direct animation IDs.

Coverage: all referenced body motions converted with mapping v3. Clips with
additional attachment channels display their body motion only. Object animation,
finger/facial animation, effects, and synchronized second-character throw playback
are not reconstructed here. Numerical/frame validation is not a visual comparison
of every move against the original game.

Each MuseumCharacter asset owns its model, bone mapping and actions. Add other
characters after importing their models and converting their own motion libraries.
