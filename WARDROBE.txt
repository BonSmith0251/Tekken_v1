WARDROBE (JIN, KAZUYA, XIAOYU)

Open Assets/Remaster/Scenes/Wardrobe.unity and press Play. The bar at the top
switches character. OUTFITS loads one of the character's complete outfit sets.
ITEMS swaps single items by slot: UPPER, LOWER, HAIR, HEAD and GLASSES; HEAD and
GLASSES can be None. Any swap shows "Custom outfit"; choosing an outfit resets
every slot. The character plays their own stance. Right-drag orbits, the wheel
zooms, Close-up frames the head, and Space pauses the stance.

COLORS
The COLORS panel (right) recolors the item worn in the selected slot the way the
game's customization does: up to four color slots (Color01..04) chosen per pixel
by the item's color mask; hair has one Hair color. Edit opens a palette and RGB
sliders; Game default resets a slot. Colors are per item and per color slot,
change the model live, and are saved by "Save items" with the ticks. Every scene
that dresses a character from saved items uses them.
Defaults are the game's material colors, including colors a material inherits
from its parent material (previously ignored, which left most common items
grey); hair defaults to black. Items without a color mask have no color options.
Recoloring re-tints the untinted texture on the GPU (Resources/CostumeTint.shader)
into a per-model copy of the material; the imported textures are unchanged.

SAVED ITEMS (USED IN EVERY OTHER SCENE)
Tick the box beside any item (or None for HEAD/GLASSES); slot tabs show how many
are ticked, and a * on a character's name means unsaved ticks. "Save items"
saves every character with changes. Outside the wardrobe, characters wear only
saved items: one random saved item per slot in LocalVersus/LocalVersus2 (per
session, both fighters), staging_test (one outfit per session, kept across takes)
and the Character Museum, where the preset costume list is replaced by the saved
outfit and a "Shuffle saved items" button. Unsaved items and the preset outfits
are ignored there. HEAD/GLASSES with nothing saved stay empty; an UPPER, LOWER or
HAIR slot with nothing saved keeps the model's item and logs a warning, since a
character cannot go without one. The console logs each outfit chosen.
Selections are saved by item name (so they survive rebuilds) to
Assets/Remaster/Resources/FightWardrobe/<code>.json: jin.json, kazuya.json,
xia.json. A player build saves its own changes under persistentDataPath. The
wardrobe assets are found through the editor asset database, so player builds
need them placed under Resources/Wardrobe first. Cloth physics runs only in the
Wardrobe scene; Xiaoyu's museum keeps her own hair and skirt dynamics.

Cloth & hair physics (on by default) simulates what the character wears: Unity
Cloth on the sections the game itself simulates as APEX cloth, and springs on the
_OPT helper bone chains (hair strands, tails, belts and armor flaps). Cloth items:
Jin's Long Coat, School Uniform and Emperor jackets and Tattered / Devil pieces;
Kazuya's Default 1P coat and Karate Gi trousers; Xiaoyu's Default 1P coat and
feathers, Classic, Formal and School Uniform skirts and shirt, and Panda shirt.
The toggle shows the live cloth and spring counts.

Inventory: each character's customization table,
Character/Item/CharacterItem/<JIN|KAZ|XIA>. Every CharacterItem (CI_*) whose
ItemPosition is one of the five slots and that names a mesh:
  Jin     22 upper, 19 lower, 18 hair, 23 head, 33 glasses
  Kazuya  20 upper, 19 lower, 18 hair, 24 head, 32 glasses
  Xiaoyu  16 upper, 18 lower, 21 hair, 31 head, 31 glasses
Skipped: entries without a mesh (CI_*_bdu_t_yamasa_y, CI_kaz_hed_volcano,
CI_xia_har_1Ptest), Kazuya's six developer cloth-test sheets, and Xiaoyu's
CI_xia_har_1p_old, which uses the same mesh as her Default 1P hair.
Common items (Item/Meshes/cmn, fitted per character) are marked *. Head items
with a DependencyMesh swap the default hair for the trimmed variant the game uses
under that item; other hairs are left as is. Glasses are shared static meshes on
the skeleton's Head_Socket with the character's own ItemOffsets transform, baked
onto the head bone.

Approximations: lens opacity comes from the game's parent materials, so lenses
use a fixed clear tint (sunglasses a dark tint). Common items keep their default
customization colors, mostly neutral greys. Hair-dependent glasses offsets
(IOV_* assets) and combination mesh variants other than the hat hair are not
applied, so some pairs may clip. Full-body, full-head and accessory items are
not included. Physics uses Unity Cloth and simple springs with settings adapted
from the Xiaoyu museum setup; the game's own APEX cloth assets and physics assets
(max distances, constraints, collision bodies) are not decoded, so cloth freedom
is estimated from each garment's height below the waist.

Rebuild one character (code jin, kaz or xia; folder Jin, Kazuya or Xiaoyu):
  UnityRemaster/export_wardrobe.py --character <code>
  resolve_material_colors.py --source Assets/Remaster/Editor/<Folder>WardrobeSource
  prepare_jin_costume_materials.py --source Assets/Remaster/Editor/<Folder>WardrobeSource --out Assets/Remaster/<Folder>Wardrobe/Textures
  (outfit sets: resolve_material_colors.py, then prepare ... --tint-only, adding
  --baked-when-complete for JinCostumeSource and XiaoyuSource; images untouched)
Colors only: Remaster > Rebuild <Name> wardrobe, or the Temp/tints-<code>-wardrobe flag.
then Remaster > Rebuild <Name> wardrobe (writes Assets/Remaster/Wardrobe/<Folder>Wardrobe.asset)
and Remaster > Create wardrobe scene. export_jin_wardrobe.py still runs Jin's export.
Remaster > Rendering > Capture wardrobes checks every item and writes contact
sheets to Validation/Wardrobe/<code>-<slot>.png with results in <code>-wardrobe.json.
