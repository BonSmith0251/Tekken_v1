JIN WARDROBE

Open Assets/Remaster/Scenes/Wardrobe.unity and press Play.
OUTFITS loads one of Jin's seven complete outfit sets. ITEMS swaps single items
by slot: UPPER, LOWER, HAIR, HEAD and GLASSES; HEAD and GLASSES can be None.
Any swap shows "Custom outfit"; choosing an outfit resets every slot.
Jin plays his own stance. Right-drag orbits, the wheel zooms, Close-up frames
the head, and Space pauses the stance.
SAVED ITEMS (USED IN EVERY OTHER SCENE)
Tick the box beside any item (or None for HEAD/GLASSES); slot tabs show how many
are ticked. Press "Save fight items" to save. Outside the wardrobe, Jin wears only
saved items: one random saved item per slot in LocalVersus/LocalVersus2 (per
session), staging_test (one outfit per session, kept across takes) and the
Character Museum, where his preset costume list is replaced by the saved outfit
and a "Shuffle saved items" button. Unsaved items and the seven preset outfits
are ignored there. HEAD/GLASSES with nothing saved stay empty; an UPPER, LOWER or
HAIR slot with nothing saved keeps the model's item and logs a warning, since Jin
cannot go without one. The console logs each outfit chosen.
The selection is saved to Assets/Remaster/Resources/FightWardrobe/jin.json (by item
name, so it survives rebuilds); a player build saves its own changes under
persistentDataPath. The wardrobe asset is found through the editor asset database,
so player builds need it placed under Resources/Wardrobe first. Cloth physics runs
only in the Wardrobe scene.

Cloth & hair physics (on by default) simulates what Jin wears: Unity Cloth on the
sections the game itself simulates as APEX cloth (Long Coat, School Uniform and
Emperor jackets, Tattered / Devil hood, cloth and trousers), and springs on the
_OPT helper bone chains (hair strands, hachimaki and Santa hat tails, belts and
armor flaps). The toggle shows the live cloth and spring counts.

Inventory: Jin's customization table, Character/Item/CharacterItem/JIN. Every
CharacterItem (CI_*) whose ItemPosition is one of the five slots and that names
a mesh: 22 upper, 19 lower, 18 hair, 23 head and 33 glasses. Common items
(Item/Meshes/cmn, fitted per character) are marked *. CI_jin_bdu_t_yamasa_y has
no mesh in the installed data and is skipped.
Head items with a DependencyMesh swap Jin's default hair for the trimmed variant
the game uses under that item (jin_har_1p_A to _I); other hairs are left as is.
Glasses are shared static meshes on the skeleton's Head_Socket with Jin's own
ItemOffsets transform, baked onto the head bone.

Approximations: lens opacity comes from the game's parent materials, so lenses
use a fixed clear tint (sunglasses a dark tint). Common items keep their default
customization colors, mostly neutral greys. Hair-dependent glasses offsets
(IOV_* assets) and combination mesh variants other than the hat hair are not
applied, so some pairs may clip. Full-body, full-head and accessory items are
not included. Physics uses Unity Cloth and simple springs with settings adapted
from the Xiaoyu museum setup; the game's own APEX cloth assets and physics assets
(max distances, constraints, collision bodies) are not decoded, so cloth freedom
is estimated from each garment's height below the waist.

Rebuild: UnityRemaster/export_jin_wardrobe.py, then
  prepare_jin_costume_materials.py --source Assets/Remaster/Editor/JinWardrobeSource --out Assets/Remaster/JinWardrobe/Textures
then Remaster > Rebuild Jin wardrobe (writes Assets/Remaster/Wardrobe/JinWardrobe.asset)
and Remaster > Create Jin wardrobe scene.
Remaster > Rendering > Capture Jin wardrobe checks every item and writes
contact sheets to Validation/Wardrobe.
