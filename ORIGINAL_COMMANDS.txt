ORIGINAL COMMANDS - KAZUYA, JIN AND XIAOYU

Choose the fighters on the VersusGame component (Player One / Player Two Fighter:
Kazuya, Jin or Xiaoyu; LocalVersus2 defaults to Jin versus Xiaoyu). Each fighter
plays its own entrance and victory takes, moveset and Rage Art camera.

LocalVersus and LocalVersus2 now run the installed game's own movesets. Every
input goes through the fighter's original command graph (kazmot.bin/jinmot.bin):
cancel lists, group cancels, input sequences, frame windows, starting frames,
requirements and move transitions. Press F1 in a match for each fighter's
command list, taken from the game's movelist.

CONTROLS (Tekken 7 PC defaults; directions are relative to facing)
  P1   W A S D move          U = 1   I = 2   J = 3   K = 4
  P2   Arrow keys            Num 4 = 1   Num 5 = 2   Num 1 = 3   Num 2 = 4
                             (no number pad: O = 1  P = 2  L = 3  ; = 4)
  Pads Left stick; X = 1, Y = 2, A = 3, B = 4 (original pad layout)
  Hold back to guard high/mid, down-back to guard low. f,f dash; b,b backdash;
  tap up/down to sidestep; hold down to crouch. F2 toggles CPU auto-fight.

WHAT IS ORIGINAL
  - Which move every input produces, strings and their windows, motion inputs
    (f,n,d,d/f), stances (Mental Alertness, Zenshin), Rage Art/Drive, slide inputs.
  - Move timing: each move's animation, recovery entry (0x8000) and transitions.
  - Movement: walk, dash, backdash and sidestep distances follow each clip's
    own root travel; attacks step in as the originals do.
  - Active frames, damage, hit level (high/mid/low/special low), counter-hit
    and juggle hit conditions, and hit/block/counter reaction moves. As in the
    game, the defender plays the reaction from the attacker's moveset and returns
    to its own moveset at a standard state (stance, crouch, downed).
  - Knockdowns: the move's vulnerability state and airborne window decide how a
    fighter can be hit. Downed fighters are only reached by lows (downed reaction
    slot); airborne bodies are juggled (juggle hit conditions, or the standard
    airborne hit sDw_AIR00_) and cannot guard. Falls land in the original down
    states and getups are the original moves: up = stand, f/b = roll up, 3/4 =
    getup kicks, 1 = side roll, 1+2 or 3+4 on landing = tech roll.
  - KO: condition 135 ("on death") sends the defeated fighter into the original
    death moves (fatal_s standing KO fall, dDm_death00/D from the ground, then the
    final lying pose); the round waits for the fall to finish. Move changes blend
    over 5 frames, like the original animation system, instead of snapping.
  - Backturned: the body heading comes from each clip's hip yaw; property 0x81A3
    holds a turn between moves, backturned moves (R_) are animated already turned,
    and conditions 72/73 and 80/81 (backturned, by side) follow it. The side split
    of 80/81 is fitted.
  - Health scale: 170.
  - Impact camera cues from the move properties: four shake levels (0x8001-4 and
    0x8006-9, on knockdowns, floor bounces, screws and Rage Arts), the preset zoom on
    airborne reactions (0x84CC), the Rage Art cinematic camera (0x8439/0x843A: bank-4
    look-at tracks in <chr>rotsm.arc, chosen by screen side, blended back by the
    track's own weight) and the Rage Art slow-down of the opponent (0x828C, with the
    attacker's 0x828D immunity). Rage Art hits hold the victim until the attacker's
    move ends, then the original reaction plays.

APPROXIMATIONS / NOT YET RECOVERED
  - Strike reach is a fixed 1.15 m along the fight line (no hitbox volumes recovered).
  - Fighters face each other in 3D; sidesteps move across the fight line and the
    camera stays perpendicular to it. Conditions 150/151 (screen side), 289 and 114
    (own frame, sidestep taps) were inferred from the sidestep cancels.
  - Impact magnitudes (shake amplitude, zoom amount), the slow-down rate per level,
    and the final-blow slow motion with close-up are approximations; the data names
    the cue and its timing only. The K.O. call and its timing, the KO slow motion
    (longer on the match-deciding blow) and the delay before the victory take are
    presentation choices, not recovered data. The on-hit Rage Art cinematic (0x843D presets) and
    its multi-hit damage are not reproduced.
  - Getting up needs an input in the original. Here the CPU picks an original
    getup after a short pause, and a player who stays down without input for 45
    frames stands up in place (a convenience, not original behaviour).
  - Throws, parries, power crush, wall/floor breaks, juggle gravity and air
    hitboxes, pushback values and wall interactions are not simulated.
  - Combos that need the opponent airborne (movelist "Sample Combo" entries)
    do not connect as in the game.
  - Character-specific reaction clips of the attacker are applied to the
    defender's skeleton (same 19 bones); shared reactions use the defender's
    own conversion.
  - The 60-frame input buffer and the unlabeled requirement defaults were fitted
    to the game's movelist demos (see VALIDATION).

VALIDATION
  Tools/CommandGraph/command_reference.py is the reference interpreter. Its test
  replays every movelist demo the game ships (the frame-by-frame inputs the
  in-game demo uses) and checks one move per button press:
      Jin 66/66 plain commands, Kazuya 71/71, Xiaoyu 115/115 (combos needing
      setups excluded).
  Remaster > Validate original command graphs (or Temp/validate-command-graph)
  replays the same demos through the runtime C# interpreter, requires identical
  move traces to the reference, runs CPU versus CPU, and checks guard rules.
  Report: Validation/CommandGraph/command-graph-unity.json.

REGENERATE
  From the project folder (adjust paths):
    python Tools/CommandGraph/export_command_graph.py --moveset <tkdata>/mothead/bin/jinmot.bin
        --catalog Assets/Remaster/Museum/JinFull/catalog.json --character-id 6 --stance 1442
        --movelist References/CommandGraph/jin-movelist.json --out Assets/Remaster/Resources/CommandGraph/jin.json
    (Kazuya: kazmot.bin, KazuyaFull, --character-id 9 --stance 1435, kaz-movelist.json, kaz.json)
    (Xiaoyu: xiamot.bin, XiaoyuFull, --character-id 5 --stance 1428, xia-movelist.json, xia.json)
    cd Tools/CommandGraph && python test_movelist_demos.py --graph ../../Assets/Remaster/Resources/CommandGraph/jin.json
        --movelist ../../References/CommandGraph/jin-movelist.json --opponent 9
        --report ../../Validation/CommandGraph/jin-reference.json
  Rage Art cameras: python Tools/CommandGraph/export_fight_cameras.py --graph <graph json>
        --archive <tkdata>/mothead/mask/<chr>rotsm.arc --code <chr> --out Assets/Remaster/Resources/FightCameras
  Requirement condition names follow the TKMovesets Tekken 7 editor labels
  (https://github.com/Kiloutre/TKMovesets).
