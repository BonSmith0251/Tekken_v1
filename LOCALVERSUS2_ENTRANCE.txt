LocalVersus2 entrance implementation

Open Assets/Remaster/Scenes/LocalVersus2.unity, or use Remaster > Open LocalVersus2 entrance scene, then press Play. The scene starts with manual Jin (P1) versus Kazuya (P2). It shows Jin's entrance shot, Kazuya's entrance shot, a face-off, READY, then FIGHT. Enter skips the sequence. The existing controls apply after FIGHT. The match timer and combat simulation remain stopped through the entrance.

Jin starts directly with the original `jz_s00` sequence (move 2218, character_0661.bytes), playing all 255 frames with its matching camera. His complete shot lasts 255 frames.

Kazuya starts directly with `kz_s06_7CS` (move 2247, character_0700.bytes), playing all 316 frames with its matching camera. His complete shot lasts 316 frames. Both entrance clips retain their original root movement so the actor stays aligned with the source camera trajectory.

Original camera data is recovered from bank 4 of jinrotsm.arc and kazrotsm.arc. Move property 33845 links Jin's action to track 104 (0x4068) and Kazuya's action to track 120 (0x4078). Each source track has exactly the same duration as its body clip. EntranceCameraTrack samples native position, XYZ Euler rotation, and lens channels at 60 fps, preserving cuts without smoothing. The native camera looks along negative Z; the Unity conversion uses the same heading/reflection as the body retargeter, converts millimeters to meters, and interprets the lens channel as horizontal FOV in radians. These conventions still need a visual comparison with the original game. Tools/EntranceCamera/source-manifest.json records source hashes and mapping evidence; import_entrance_cameras.py regenerates the resource bytes.

The extra combat walk-ins have been removed: their raised-guard walking poses were not part of the original entrance sequences. Each entrance animation and camera now start together at frame zero, with fixed actor anchors and movement supplied by the clip. After Kazuya's final sample, an authored 45-frame face-off blend restores gameplay position, rotation, and FOV; READY lasts 60 frames and FIGHT lasts 35. The combat camera does not write during the entrance. Enter skips immediately, clears both motion overrides, and restores the gameplay camera and lens. Total entrance time is 11.85 seconds; smoke-test capture/exit timing includes this duration.

Build menu: Remaster > Build LocalVersus2 Windows player. Output: Builds/LocalVersus2/LocalVersus2.exe.

Lens and aspect audit: entrance rendering now uses a centered 16:9 viewport, including in a resized Unity Game view. This reference aspect is consistent with the installed game's DesiredScreenWidth=1280 and DesiredScreenHeight=720; it has not been measured from the original game's live projection matrix. Black bars fill unused screen space and entrance UI scales to the same viewport. The native FOV samples remain authoritative for zoom, interpreted as horizontal radians consistently with Unreal's horizontal FOV convention and the local TekkenDirect camera reference. Unity now uses an equivalent physical lens: 36 x 20.25 mm sensor, zero lens shift, gate fit None, focalLength = 36 / (2 * tan(horizontalFov / 2)). These sensor dimensions are a conversion convention, not recovered original hardware. The old serialized 50 mm value was inactive while the camera used nonphysical FOV. Original viewport and lens settings are restored on skip/completion. See Tools/EntranceCamera/lens-audit.json for sampled ranges and evidence limits.

Verification: runtime scripts compile against the installed Unity assemblies. Camera tests verify body/track durations, finite samples, valid lens values, and exact equality of all exported samples with the original archives. A numerical rig projection check places each character's head inside the camera frame at the first, middle, and last samples. The integrated scene has not been visually verified in Play mode; check the original game's framing, cuts, zoom, and the skip-to-fight transition before claiming an exact reproduction.

The user confirmed normal gameplay. See Tools/EntranceCamera/gameplay-camera-capture.json.

1.777778 (16:9). The clone now uses this recovered horizontal FOV, replacing
its 38-degree vertical prototype setting. At 16:9 the vertical FOV is
39.43049 degrees and equivalent focal length is 28.25434 mm on a 36 mm gate.
The filmback/focal length are equivalences, not recovered native settings.
Gameplay shares the entrance lens controller; Ready/fight and skipping preserve
this projection. HUD and menus fit the viewport, including after resizing.
Native position and rotation samples are recorded as evidence, but the tracking
algorithm, distance rules and exceptional cinematic lenses are not reconstructed.
