LocalVersus2 entrance implementation

Open Assets/Remaster/Scenes/LocalVersus2.unity, or use Remaster > Open LocalVersus2 entrance scene, then press Play. The scene starts with manual Jin (P1) versus Kazuya (P2). It shows Jin's entrance shot, Kazuya's entrance shot, a face-off, READY, then FIGHT. Enter skips the sequence. The existing controls apply after FIGHT. The match timer and combat simulation remain stopped through the entrance.

Jin now plays his imported `Jz_sWALKF` motion for the first 60 presentation frames while entering, then his imported `Jz_zansin00` stance motion for the next 60 frames. These are original character action clips arranged into a new entrance sequence; the game's original cinematic entrance clip has not been recovered. Skipping or completing the entrance clears the presentation override before Fight begins.

Kazuya plays his imported `Kz_sWALKF` motion while moving into place for the first 60 frames of his shot, then the separate `kz_s06_7CS` sequence (move 2247, character_0700.bytes). This replaces the ordinary guard idle previously used as his entrance action. The full 316-frame sequence plays once at 60 fps, with Kazuya stationary and the camera staying on him; his shot lasts 376 frames before the face-off. The duration is read from the motion asset so the sequence is not truncated. His presentation override is cleared when the entrance ends or is skipped. This clip's visual match to the original versus entrance still needs verification in the editor.

The extracted cooked scripts identify distinct OnCharacterEntrance, OnOpponentEntrance, OnBattleBegin, and OnRoundActionStart events in some original game components (for example the exported functions of BP_mig_exp_apple). They do not provide a readable complete sequence or converted Jin/Kazuya intro cinematics. This scene implements that event order as a new Unity presentation using the current imported fighters and idle motion. Shot length and movement are provisional and should be matched to direct original game capture before describing them as exact Tekken 7 timing.
