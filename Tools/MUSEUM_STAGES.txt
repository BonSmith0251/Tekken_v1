Original stages and env_scene

34 layouts are extracted from the installed game's geometry maps (stgNN_geom.umap)
plus their _reload and _demo sublevels, which hold breakable floors, fences and
fixed pieces such as stg08a's ring. Destructible and skeletal meshes are drawn in
their rest pose. Layouts keep their native stg identifiers, including substage variants. Each mesh keeps
its normals, tangents, UVs and material sections. Every original material instance
becomes an HDRP/Lit material (Assets/_Project/Stages/Materials) with its base colour,
normal and emissive maps exported as PNG to Assets/_Project/Stages/Textures.
Components' OverrideMaterials are applied. Each stage is placed on the original's
GAME_START marker (first floor) and turned so the marker's direction looks along +Z,
the versus camera's view; the floor under the marker (glass and water included) is
put at the fighters' feet. Stage prefabs are in
Assets/_Project/Resources/MuseumStages.

Stage particle systems (brazier fires, falling sand, dust, mist...) play on loop from
Resources/StageEffects through CascadeEffects: StageEffectMarker objects in the prefab
mark where each sits, and StageEnvironment starts the looping ones in Play (one-shot
systems are break bursts the remaster doesn't trigger). Light shafts (BP_LightBeam god
rays) are rebuilt as crossed additive quads (Resources/StageEffects/LightShaft.shader)
from their dynamic material's width, length, brightness, fade distance and mask. Meshes attached to capsule or billboard
components (stg02/26 rocks and waterfalls, god rays) are placed under those parents.

Original stage lighting: the exporter also reads _light/_lightstatic and records each
light (type, intensity, colour, radius, cone, falloff, channel, mobility), the sky light
(its cubemap, saved as Stages/Lighting/<name>.t7sky), the height fog, the stage's
unbound post-process volume and its WindDirectionalSource. Blueprint lights take their
values from the blueprint template. The importer puts real HDRP lights on the light
nodes (LC_CHARA_BOTH lights only light fighters) and builds Stages/Lighting/<stg>_Lighting
.asset (fixed exposure EV 0, ACES, HDRI or gradient sky, height fog, bloom, saturation,
vignette, AO, indirect intensity). StageLighting on the prefab turns that on and the
scene's Render Settings rig off while the stage is shown (StageEnvironment.Spawn/Despawn).

Foliage wind: StageWind sways ivy, vines, palms, leaves and plants from the stage's
wind source (hanging ivy most at its free ends, plants at their tips, plus a small
flutter), on the CPU within a 120k-vertex budget nearest the fighters. 16 stages have
foliage; Temp/verify-stage-wind writes Validation/Stages/wind.json (offsets in metres).

Lighting approximations (set by eye): baked-only (static) lights stand in as shadowless
realtime lights (nearest 96, lumens x0.25 capped at 2000, and together at most 6 lux at the
fighters: stg18 packs 666 bake lights, ~52 lux; stg13 ~180; stg90 ~77; Validation/Stages/
baked-lights.json); suns above 20 lux are outliers and get a typical 8 lux (stg18's is 200); sky-light intensity is capped at 1.5; sky reflections at 35% because
the original's reflection captures aren't converted; wind strength is capped at 0.5.
Not converted: lightmaps, reflection captures, light functions, flicker/pulse animation.

Approximations: soft particles (depth fade) are drawn at 30% opacity, the flame shape
mask's orientation is assumed (tip up), and light shafts use 40% of their brightness
against this scene's dimmer lighting. By request, stg02/stg26's jungle mist
(PS_EF_STG02_Mist, HDR red-orange in the data) is tinted pale green-white
(TINT_OVERRIDES in Tools/StageEffects/export_stage_effects.py).

Not converted: shader graphs (layer blends, vertex-colour mixes, water, panners),
baked lightmaps, reflection captures, animated scenery, crowds, particle modules
the decoder lists under "limitations" in Resources/StageEffects/report.json, and
breakable-stage gameplay. Materials that still have no texture are listed, with the
reason, under "untexturedMaterials" in Assets/_Project/Stages/Source/report.json.

Stage scenes (editing and lightmap baking): Assets/_Project/Scenes/Stages/<stg>.unity, one per
stage, built by StageSceneSetup. Each holds a linked instance of the stage prefab (re-imports
update it) placed on its match start, the stage's sky/fog/post volume (<stg>/Volume.asset) with
its static lighting sky, every light the original only baked switched to Unity Baked mode (suns
Mixed), a 676-probe light-probe grid and a baked reflection probe over the fight area, a camera
on the match start and LightingSettings (<stg>/Lighting.lighting: GPU progressive, 2 texels/m,
2048 maps, non-directional, AO; bounce tuned: indirect x2.5, albedo boost 1.5, 4 bounces, suns'
indirect x2, 4x texel density within 30 m of the match start). A tuned stg01 bake took 9 minutes
and 280 MB. Opaque and cut-out surfaces contribute to the bake; transparent
cards, helper surfaces and sky domes don't; foliage, small props and meshes without a lightmap
UV take light probes. Lightmap UVs are Unreal's own (channel 1) where valid, else unwrapped.
Fly navigation: each stage scene has a "Fly Navigator" (StageNavigator). Press Play and fly the
scene camera anywhere, through walls and over the roof (no collision): WASD move, Q/E down/up,
Shift faster, Ctrl slower, right-drag look, scroll changes speed, R back to the start view,
H hide help.
To bake: open one stage scene on its own (File > Open Scene) and use Remaster > Stages > Bake
open stage scene (or Lighting > Generate Lighting); it refuses while other scenes are open.
The scenes are not in Build Settings; versus and env_scene still use the prefabs, so baked
lighting doesn't reach them yet. Lightmaps take disk space (D: had 13 GB free on 2026-10-26).

Choosing environments: open Assets/_Project/Scenes/env_scene.unity and press Play.
Alisa and Jin stand on the previewed stage. Click a name to preview it, tick the ones
versus may use, then Save selection (Ctrl+S). The choice is stored in
Assets/_Project/Resources/EnvironmentSelection.asset (in a player build, in
persistentDataPath/environment-selection.json). LocalVersus and LocalVersus2 play
each match on a random ticked environment; with none ticked, or only "Remaster arena",
they use the generated arena. The museum no longer has a STAGE control.

Regenerate (the editor can stay open):
  python Tools/import_museum_stages.py            (all stages; or list stg names)
  python Tools/StageEffects/export_stage_effects.py   (particle systems the stages use)
  create Temp/import-stages                       (MuseumStageImporter.Import)
  create Temp/create-env-scene                    (rebuild env_scene)
  create Temp/verify-stage-wind                  (foliage sway offsets; stage names per line)
  create Temp/create-stage-scenes                (Scenes/Stages/<stg>.unity; stage names per
                                                   line, "force" rebuilds existing scenes)
  create Temp/fix-lightmap-uvs                    (unwrap meshes missing lightmap UVs)
  create Temp/bake-stage-scene                    (bake the one open stage scene)
  create Temp/add-stage-navigator                 (add/rename the fly navigator in existing scenes)
  create Temp/retune-open-stage                   (apply the bounce/texel tuning to the open scene)
  create Temp/capture-open-stage                  (render the open stage scene; lines
                                                   "label yaw pitch dist [baked-only]")
  create Temp/capture-stages                      (renders to Validation/Stages; one
                                                   stage name per line, or a default set;
                                                   "stg01 view <yaw> <pitch> <dist> <label>"
                                                   adds one stage-only view)
